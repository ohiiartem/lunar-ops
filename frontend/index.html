    Why the lunar south pole is special

    • At the lunar south pole the Sun stays within a few degrees of the horizon. A single hill can hide it.
    • Earth barely moves in the lunar sky, because the Moon always shows the same face to Earth. So Earth visibility usually depends on the terrain in that one direction.
    • The Moon has no atmosphere: no refraction and no twilight glow. When the Sun is hidden, it is dark.
