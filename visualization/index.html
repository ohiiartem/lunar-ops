What does this mean?

At the Moon’s south pole, the Sun stays low in the sky and nearby hills can block it. Earth usually moves very little in the lunar sky, so the hills in its direction matter.

Glossary
Azimuth (AZ)
Compass direction in degrees, measured clockwise from north.
Elevation (EL)
Angle above or below the level plane at the observer.
Terrain horizon
Angle of the highest obstruction in that direction, within the available terrain data.
Geometric visibility
Whether the object clears the horizon in the model.
Solar upper limb
The top edge of the Sun’s disk.
Sunset / Earthset
The Sun’s top edge or Earth’s center drops below the selected horizon.
